# My Favorite Series: *Stranger Things*

![Stranger Things Banner]

---

## Overview
**Stranger Things** is a science fiction–horror series created by *The Duffer Brothers*.  
It is set in the 1980s in the fictional town of **Hawkins, Indiana**, where mysterious supernatural events occur.

> "Friends don't lie." – Eleven  

---

## Main Characters
- **Eleven (Millie Bobby Brown)** – A girl with telekinetic powers  
- **Mike Wheeler (Finn Wolfhard)** – Eleven’s close friend and leader of the group  
- **Dustin Henderson (Gaten Matarazzo)** – The humorous and smart one  
- **Lucas Sinclair (Caleb McLaughlin)** – Brave and loyal  
- **Jim Hopper (David Harbour)** – The town’s police chief  

---

## Why I Love It
1. Amazing 80s nostalgia 🎵  
2. Strong character development 💪  
3. Engaging mystery and suspense 🔦  
4. Perfect mix of friendship, fear, and fantasy
